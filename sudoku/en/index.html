Sudoku JANG

A clean screen and a crisp board. Five levels of Sudoku with auto-save and records.

What it does

  • Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert and Hell. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.
  • The same row, column, box and matching digits are gently highlighted so you can follow your reasoning at a glance.
  • A large number pad with notes, erase and undo.
  • Close the app mid-game and it is saved automatically. Pick up where you left off next time.
  • Best clear times per difficulty and your recent games.
  • Available in 33 languages, including English and Korean.
  • No server. Your game records stay on your device.

Ads and purchases

  • An interstitial ad is shown when you start a new game: once for the first game after launch, then at most once every 15 minutes. No ads appear during play.
  • Buy Remove Ads once in Settings and ads never come back. On a new device, use Settings > Restore Purchases.
