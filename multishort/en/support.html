FAQ

Instagram won't connect

The Instagram API can only post to professional (business or creator) accounts, and the account must be linked to a Facebook Page. Switch to a professional account in the Instagram app's settings, link a Facebook Page, then connect again. If you decline any of the permissions while connecting, uploads won't work.

My iPhone asks me to allow tracking

On iPhone, Meta grants Instagram and Facebook upload permissions only when "Allow Apps to Request to Track" is on. Turn on multishort under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, then connect again. YouTube and TikTok work without it.

How does TikTok work?

When the other uploads finish, tap "Send to TikTok app" (틱톡 앱으로 보내기) and pick TikTok in the share sheet. The caption is copied to the clipboard, so paste it in the TikTok app and post. The TikTok app must be installed.

When do ads appear?

A rewarded ad plays once when you start an upload, and the upload starts after you watch it to the end. If you close it early, nothing is uploaded. Retrying only the failed uploads shows no ad. With the ad-free subscription, uploads start right away.

The upload stopped partway

Switching to another app or turning off the screen during an upload can interrupt it. Come back to the App and tap "Retry failed only" (실패한 곳만 다시 올리기) to re-upload just the ones that failed.

How do I cancel the subscription?

On iPhone, go to Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. On Android, go to Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. If you reinstalled the App or changed devices, tap "Restore purchases" (구매 복원) on the Remove Ads tab.

How do I disconnect?

Tap "Disconnect" (연결 해제) on the App's Connect tab to delete the connection data stored on the device. How to revoke each platform's permissions is in the Privacy Policy.
