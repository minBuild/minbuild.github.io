Snapshot 2003 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Snapshot 2003 is a camera app. It is built so that your photos and videos stay with you.

What we collect

Snapshot 2003 itself collects nothing. The developer runs no server and receives no personal data, photos, videos or usage data from the app.

  • No accounts. There is no sign-in.
  • No server. The app does not send your photos, videos or usage data to the developer.
  • Ads in the free version. The free version of Snapshot 2003 shows one Google AdMob banner at the bottom of the Memory Card screen and a short full-screen ad every 3 shots with the DIGI '04 camera. If you choose to, you can also watch a rewarded ad to take one shot with the FLIP camera. Google Mobile Ads is the only third-party SDK in the app. Snapshot 2003 Pro is ad-free.

Data Google collects for ads (free version only)

When an ad is shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect the following on Google's behalf, as described in Google's App Store data disclosure for the SDK:

  • Device ID: the advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking) or other app- or developer-scoped device identifiers. Used for advertising and analytics.
  • Advertising data: for example, which ads you have seen. Used for advertising and analytics.
  • Product interaction: for example, taps on and views of ads. Used to measure and improve ad performance.
  • Coarse location: your IP address, used to estimate your general location.
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance data such as launch time or hang rate. Used to fix and improve the SDK, and also for advertising and analytics.

Google handles this data under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choices

  • Tracking permission (iOS). The free version may ask whether Snapshot 2003 can track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. Every feature works the same whether you allow it or not. If you don't allow it, ads are not personalized with your advertising identifier.
  • EEA and UK consent. If you are in the EEA or the UK, Google's consent form asks for your choices before ads load. You can change them later in Settings › Privacy options.
  • Pro. Snapshot 2003 Pro is ad-free. For Pro users the ads SDK is never started, so it collects nothing.

Your photos and videos

  • Photos and videos you take are stored in the app's own storage on your device ("Memory Card").
  • If you allow it, a copy is also saved to your Photos library. Snapshot 2003 only asks for permission to add to Photos; it cannot read your library.
  • When you share a photo, video or album, it goes only to the destination you choose in the iOS share sheet.
  • Deleting an item in Snapshot 2003 removes the app's copy. The copy in Photos stays.
  • Photos and videos are never sent to Google or anyone else by the app.

Camera and microphone

  • The camera is used only while the app is open to show the viewfinder and take pictures.
  • The microphone is used only while you record with the camcorder, to record sound for that video.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Snapshot 2003 receives only the information needed to unlock Pro on your device; it never sees your payment details.

Children

Snapshot 2003 is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children. Ads in the free version are limited to content rated for teens and older.
