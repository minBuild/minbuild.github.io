Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Sudoku JANG (the "app") is a Sudoku puzzle game made by minBuild (the "developer"). The app has no accounts or sign-in, and the developer does not receive or store any information about you on a server. However, the companies that provide the ads and payments built into the app (Google and Apple) process information as described below.

1. Information kept only on your device

InformationUsed forStorage and transfer
Game in progress The digits, notes and elapsed time of the puzzle you are solving, so you can resume it Stored only on your device. Never sent off the device.
Game records Clear times, mistakes and dates per difficulty for the Records screen Stored only on your device. Never sent off the device.
Settings and purchase status Preferences such as theme, and whether Remove Ads was purchased Stored only on your device. Payments are handled by the App Store or Google Play.

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo library.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows interstitial ads from Google AdMob. An ad appears when you start a new game: once for the first game after launch, then at most once every 15 minutes. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers: an identifier scoped to this app, and, if you allow it, the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate region
  • Interactions with ads such as views and clicks, and diagnostics such as app launches and errors

On iOS, the app asks for permission to track when you first open it. If you do not allow it, the app requests non-personalized ads and the advertising identifier is not used. Every feature of the app works the same either way. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, you can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Settings > Google > Ads.

In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent message is shown on first launch. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy options.

How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Once you purchase Remove Ads, the app no longer loads the advertising component.

3. Payments (Apple, Google)

The Remove Ads in-app purchase is processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play. The developer never receives payment details such as card numbers. The app stores only whether the purchase was made.

4. Third parties

Apart from sections 2 and 3, your information is not shared with or processed by third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

The developer does not retain any personal information. Game progress and records stored on your device are deleted when you delete the app. If device backup (such as iCloud) is enabled, app data may be included in the backup. Purchases remain on your Apple or Google account and can be restored with "Restore Purchases" in the app. Information collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

6. Your choices

  • Tracking (iOS): Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Advertising ID (Android): Settings > Google > Ads to reset it or opt out of personalization.
  • Ad consent (where applicable): Settings > Privacy options in the app.
  • Delete everything stored on your device: delete the app.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
