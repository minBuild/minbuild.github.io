One Verse

Flip a card once a day and receive one Bible verse.

  • Once a day, flip the card to draw today's verse. Reopen it the same day and you get the same verse; a new card opens after midnight.
  • Everyone gets their own order of 249 verses, with no repeats for 249 days.
  • Read in Korean, English, Spanish and Japanese, or show two languages together.
  • Read the whole Bible in four translations in the Bible tab and save the verses that stay with you.
  • Share a verse as an image in the app's own design.
  • No sign-in, no ads, no tracking. Your history stays on your device.

Translations

Korean Revised Version (1961), World English Bible, Reina-Valera 1909 and Kougo-yaku (1954/1955). All are in the public domain or out of copyright.

Support the app

Every core feature is free. A one-time tip is simply a gift of support. Monthly support (an auto-renewing subscription) adds supporter card themes, with a new seasonal theme on the 1st of each month, and alternate app icons.
