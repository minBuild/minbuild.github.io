Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

multishort (the "App") uploads a short-form video from your phone to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels at once, and hands it off to the TikTok app. There is no sign-up and no server run by the developer (minBuild). Your videos and account information go directly from your device to each platform (Google, Meta); the developer does not receive or store your information.

The App reads only the videos you pick from your photo library. It does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone. The App contains no analytics tools.

2. YouTube (YouTube API Services)

The App uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to YouTube. When you connect YouTube, you sign in with your Google account and grant these two permissions:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload): uploads the video you choose to upload to your channel.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): reads your channel name to show which channel the video goes to.

The App uses information received through these permissions only to upload and to show your channel name. It does not share or sell it to anyone, including the developer, and does not use it for advertising. multishort's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

By using the YouTube features, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles your information.

Tapping "Disconnect" (연결 해제) on the App's Connect tab revokes the permissions the App received from Google and deletes the channel name stored on the device. You can also revoke multishort's access at any time on your Google Account's security settings page.

3. Instagram and Facebook (Meta)

Instagram and Facebook are connected with Meta's Facebook Login. The App requests the following permissions and uses them only to post Reels to the Facebook Page you choose and the Instagram professional account linked to it.

  • pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, business_management: finding the Pages you manage and the Instagram accounts linked to them
  • pages_manage_posts: posting Reels to your Facebook Page
  • instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish: identifying your Instagram account and posting Reels

Automatic app event logging and advertiser ID collection in the bundled Facebook SDK are turned off. Information Meta processes during sign-in is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy.

On iPhone, Meta grants upload permissions only when "Allow Apps to Request to Track" is allowed, so the App asks for it before connecting Instagram and Facebook. If you do not allow it, you can still use YouTube and TikTok.

4. Ads and payments

Ads (Google AdMob)

Users without the ad-free subscription tap "Watch ad and upload" (광고 보고 업로드) before uploading and watch a Google AdMob rewarded ad. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (Android advertising ID; on iPhone, the IDFA if you allowed tracking) and an identifier specific to this app
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Interactions with ads such as views and clicks, and diagnostic information such as app launches and errors

Google and its advertising partners may store or read advertising identifiers or similar information on your device. In regions that require consent, such as Europe, the App asks for ad consent on first launch, and you can change it under "Ad privacy settings" (광고 개인정보 설정) on the Remove Ads tab. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. With the ad-free subscription, no ads are shown.

Subscription payments (Apple, Google, RevenueCat)

The ad-free subscription is paid through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). The developer never receives payment details such as card numbers. RevenueCat checks whether the subscription is active using store receipts, and for this processes a randomly generated app user ID, purchase history, and device and app version information. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy for details.

5. Sharing and processors

Other than sending your video and post details to the platforms you connected and chose to upload to (Google, Meta), and the ad and payment processing in section 4, the App does not share your information with third parties or have anyone process it on the developer's behalf.

6. Retention and deletion

The developer has no server and keeps none of your personal information. Connection data stored on your device can be deleted as follows:

  • "Disconnect" (연결 해제) on the App's Connect tab: deletes that platform's connection data from the device. For YouTube, it also revokes the permissions granted by Google.
  • Deleting the App: removes the App's data stored on the device.
  • Revoking Google access: remove multishort in your Google Account's security settings.
  • Revoking Meta access: remove multishort in Facebook Settings › Security and Login › Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites).

Posts you already published stay on each platform; you can delete them directly on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. The developer holds no data that needs deleting, but if you need help with deletion, contact us at the address below.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 14 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
