multishort

Upload one short-form video to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels at once.

What it does

  • Pick a video and write the title, description and hashtags once; it goes up everywhere you chose at the same time.
  • Titles and descriptions are trimmed to fit each platform's length limits.
  • YouTube connects with your Google account; Instagram and Facebook connect with Facebook Login.
  • TikTok: after the uploads finish, the video is handed off to the TikTok app through the share sheet.
  • You can retry only the uploads that failed.
  • There is no server. Videos go straight from your device to each platform, and connection data stays on your device.

Good to know

  • Instagram uploads require a professional (business or creator) account linked to a Facebook Page.
  • The free plan includes 5 automatic uploads per month; watch a short ad to the end before each one. With the Pro subscription, uploads start right away with no ads and no limit. Sending through the share sheet is always free.
  • The App's screens are in Korean.
