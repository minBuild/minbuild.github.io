Frequently asked questions

What does the free version include?

The DIGI '04 camera, up to 20 shots a day, one album a month, and a small "3MP" mark on each photo. A short full-screen ad plays every 3 shots with the DIGI '04, and a banner sits at the bottom of the Memory Card screen. On the FLIP camera you can watch an ad to take one FLIP shot.

What does Pro unlock?

Snapshot 2003 Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks the FLIP camera with no ads, removes the watermark, adds date shift, gives unlimited shots and albums, and removes the banner and full-screen ads.

I bought Pro but it's gone

If you reinstalled the app or moved to a new iPhone, open Settings (gear icon) and tap Restore purchases. The paywall also has Restore at the bottom. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro.

Where are my photos?

Every shot is kept on the in-app Memory Card. If you allow it, a copy is also saved to your Photos library. Deleting the app deletes the Memory Card, but copies in Photos stay.

Can I change the date on the stamp?

Date shift (Pro) stamps an earlier year on new photos. It changes only the stamp; the photo's metadata keeps the real date.

Why does the app ask to track?

In the free version the ad SDK may ask for tracking permission. Every feature works the same whether you allow it or not. Pro has no ads and never asks.

The viewfinder is black

Check that camera access is on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera › Snapshot 2003.
