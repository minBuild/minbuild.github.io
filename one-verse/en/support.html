FAQ

When does today's verse change?

A new card opens after midnight on your device's clock. On the same day you always get the same verse.

Does everyone get the same verse?

No. The order is set when you first open the app, so each person gets different verses, with no repeats for 249 days.

I reinstalled the app and my history is gone.

There is no server, so your history and saved verses live only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them; restoring a device backup brings them back.

Can I read the whole Bible?

Yes, in the Bible tab, in four translations. Long-press a verse to save, copy or share it. The app returns to where you were reading.

What is the difference between a one-time tip and monthly support?

A one-time tip is simply a gift and unlocks nothing. Monthly support is an auto-renewing subscription that adds supporter card themes and alternate app icons.

How do I cancel monthly support?

In Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions, or from Support › Monthly › Manage in the app. Perks remain until the paid period ends.

I changed devices and my perks are gone.

Tap "Restore purchases" at the bottom of the Support screen while signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Please request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
