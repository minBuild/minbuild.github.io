Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

One Verse (App Store name: One Verse - BIBLE, the "app") is made by minBuild (the "developer"). The app has no accounts, sign-in, servers, ads or analytics tools, and the developer does not receive or store any information about you.

1. Information kept only on your device

This information never leaves your device. It may be included in your device backup (such as iCloud) and is deleted when you delete the app.

2. Payments (Apple)

One-time tips and monthly support use Apple's App Store in-app purchase. The developer never receives payment details such as card numbers or your Apple Account. The app checks your monthly support status with the App Store on your device and does not send it to any server.

3. Sharing

When you share a verse, the app creates an image on your device and passes the image and text to the app you choose. The developer does not receive it.

4. What the app does not use

The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo library, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not track you. Bible texts are built into the app and can be read offline.

5. Third parties

Your information is not shared with or processed by third parties. Crash reports and app statistics that Apple may provide to developers, depending on your device settings, follow Apple's privacy policy and do not identify you.

6. Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Contact and changes

For privacy questions, email hayaeseong@gmail.com. If this policy changes, we will post it here and update the effective date.
