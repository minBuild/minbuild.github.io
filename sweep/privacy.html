English

  • Sweep does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no login, and no analytics, advertising, or crash SDK.
  • Your photos never leave your device. All analysis (grouping similar photos, measuring sharpness, finding exact duplicate files) runs on your iPhone.
  • Analysis results (image fingerprints, file hashes, file sizes, sharpness) are stored only in the device's Caches folder. They are excluded from iCloud backups and may be cleared by iOS.
  • Sorting records (keep, pending deletion, deletion statistics) are stored only in a local database inside the app and are removed when you delete the app.
  • Statistics and the monthly report are calculated on your device from these records and are stored only on your device.
  • Sweep does not make copies of your photos, with one exception: the "On This Day" photo widget (Sweep Pro). While it is turned on, the app keeps up to 7 downscaled copies in the App Group container on your device so the widget never needs to access your photo library. These copies never leave your device and are deleted when you turn the feature off or when Pro ends. Lock Screen widgets show only numbers, never photos.
    • These downscaled copies are excluded from iCloud and device backups.
  • The weekly reminder is a local notification scheduled on your iPhone. No server is involved, and notification permission is requested only when you turn the reminder on.
  • Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store. Sweep never receives payment information.
