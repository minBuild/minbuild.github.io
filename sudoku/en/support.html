FAQ

When do ads appear?

An interstitial ad is shown when you start a new game: once for the first game after launch, then only for new games started more than 15 minutes after the last ad. No ads for Resume, Replay or Restart, and none during play. If an ad cannot be loaded (for example, offline), the game starts without one after a moment.

Can I remove ads?

Buy Remove Ads once in Settings and ads never come back. The price shown on the purchase screen is the one that applies.

My purchase is gone

If you reinstalled the app or switched devices, tap "Restore Purchases" in Settings. You must be signed in with the same Apple (or Google) account you purchased with.

My game in progress disappeared

Closing the app mid-game saves it automatically and shows it on the home screen's Resume card. Starting a new game replaces the previous one.

Do my records move to a new device?

There is no server, so records live only on your device. Setting up a new device from a backup carries them over. Deleting the app deletes the records.

How do I change the language?

The app follows your device language. On iOS you can set a different language for just this app in Settings > Sudoku > Language. Unsupported languages fall back to English.
